The parameters are available in all BSIM4 models (level=14 or level=54) version=4.2.1 to version=4.8.
Negative capacitance values may occur, depending on the internal calculation. To compare with
measured data, please just use the absolute values of the capacitance data. For an explanation of
negative values and the basics on how capacitance values are evaluated in a BSIM model, please
refer to the book BSIM4 and MOSFET Modeling for IC Simulation by Liu and Hu, Chapt. 5.2.
