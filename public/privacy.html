TurboSpice privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Versión en español

TurboSpice is a circuit simulator that runs entirely in your browser. Circuits are simulated on your computer: they are not uploaded to any TurboSpice server.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional. If you use it, TurboSpice only asks for the drive.file permission, which gives it access only to the files the app itself creates or that you open with it, inside a "TurboSpice" folder in your Google Drive. TurboSpice can't see or modify the rest of your Drive.

  • Your Google name, e-mail and profile picture are used only to show you which account you signed in with.
  • Projects (.turbo) and .asc files are read and saved directly between your browser and Google Drive. They don't go through TurboSpice servers.
  • The access permission is stored only in your browser and is deleted when you sign out. You can revoke it at any time in your Google account settings.

TurboSpice's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data stored in your browser

TurboSpice stores in your browser's local storage the autosave of your project, the latest simulation results, recent files and your preferences. That data never leaves your computer and you can delete it by clearing the site's data.

What TurboSpice doesn't do

  • It doesn't sell, share or transfer data to third parties.
  • It doesn't use the data for advertising or to train models.
  • It doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics.

Contact

For any question, open an issue at github.com/TC-II/TurboSpice.
